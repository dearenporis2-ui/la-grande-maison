About La Grande Maison

"Give memory to the ephemeral…"

Elevate any event with our selection of tableware. Capture those milestone moments in a one-of-a-kind style — from classic white to high end china. We are proud to partner with restaurants, hotels and the best wedding planners on the Islands.

No matter the occasion, our line of tabletop items will help you create truly memorable special moments. We offer quality dinnerware rentals for your event — our selection includes plates, bowls, glasses, cutlery but also accessories, decorations, buffet items and more.

If you don't know exactly what you're seeking, let us know — we will be happy to place a custom order and check quantities for you.
